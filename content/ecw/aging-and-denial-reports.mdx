  - title: Open Reports
    detail: In ECW, go to Reports (or search for the report name in newer versions). Billing/AR categories usually contain aging and claims status reports.
  - title: Run AR Aging
    detail: Filter by payer, provider, facility, and aging bucket. Export or work directly from the list your team standardizes.
  - title: Run Denial summary
    detail: Group denials by reason, payer, and provider to spot systemic issues (eligibility, auth, coding, enrollment).
  - title: Assign actions
    detail: Convert report findings into queue work — not just a spreadsheet that sits unused.

## eBO note

Some organizations use **eBO (Business Objects)** for richer analytics and threshold alerts (for example AR > 90 days over a dollar amount). If your site has eBO, ask your lead which scheduled reports are authoritative.

## Cadence

- Weekly aging review for 60+ and 90+ buckets
- Weekly denial reason trends
- Daily queue work against assigned inventory
