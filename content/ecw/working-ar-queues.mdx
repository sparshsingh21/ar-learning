  - title: Open the AR / aging worklist
    detail: From PM reporting or work queue dashboards, open the AR aging or follow-up list your team uses (often sorted by aging bucket or payer).
  - title: Open the claim
    detail: Select the encounter/claim to view charges, balances, claim status history, and prior notes.
  - title: Record ownership
    detail: If your process assigns accounts, claim the work item or document your user ID in notes so duplicates are avoided.
  - title: Leave a next action
    detail: After research, update status, create tasks, or set a follow-up date per client SOP before leaving the account.

## Working style tips

- Prefer **oldest / highest dollar / nearest TFL** first unless your lead sets another priority
- Check whether the claim is already tasked to coding or credentialing before calling
- Keep notes in the three-part format from the [AR analyst process](/learn/ar-analyst-process)
