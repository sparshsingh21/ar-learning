whenToUse: Use whenever you finish research on an account.
steps:
  - title: Open the claim or encounter notes
    detail: Locate the billing/AR notes panel used by your site (claim notes vs patient notes — follow SOP so clinical notes are not polluted).
  - title: Paste structured notes
    detail: Enter Analysis / Research / Action / Next. Include call reference numbers and agent names when applicable.
  - title: Create or update a task
    detail: If another team must act (coding, enrollment), create a task with due date and clear ownership rather than only mentioning it in free text.
  - title: Set follow-up
    detail: Schedule the next touch based on payer turnaround (for example 7–14 days after a reconsideration) and aging risk.
---

## Note quality checklist

- Another analyst can continue without calling the payer again for the same facts
- Identifiers are accurate (claim #, ICN, call ref)
- Next owner and date are explicit
