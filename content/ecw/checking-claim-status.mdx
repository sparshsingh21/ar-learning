title: Checking Claim Status in ECW
description: Use ECW claim history and clearinghouse integrations before making a payer call.
section: ecw
tags: [ecw, claim-status, clearinghouse]
order: 3
updated: 2026-08-27
whenToUse: Use before every payer call to avoid asking for information already on screen.
steps:
  - title: Open claim status / history
    detail: From the claim, open status history to see submission dates, payer claim numbers, rejects, and remits already posted.
  - title: Review clearinghouse response
    detail: Confirm whether the claim was accepted by the clearinghouse and forwarded to the payer, or rejected with an edit.
  - title: Capture identifiers
    detail: Copy payer claim number, patient member ID as billed, NPI/Tax ID used, and billed amount into your notes before calling.
  - title: Decide portal vs phone
    detail: If status is clear (paid, denied with code, rejected), act in-system. Call only when status is missing, conflicting, or needs appeal/COB detail.
---

## Common pitfalls

- Searching the wrong DOS or wrong subscriber ID format
- Missing that a **corrected claim** replaced the original
- Calling on a rejection that only needs data fix and resubmit
