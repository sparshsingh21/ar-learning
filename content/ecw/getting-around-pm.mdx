## Important

ECW layouts differ by version and whether you are on a **Central Billing Office (CBO)** configuration. Treat the steps above as a template — replace screenshots with your environment’s PHI-redacted captures in `public/ecw/`.

## Guardrails

- Never leave PHI in training screenshots
- Confirm whether you use Jelly Bean / Resource Schedule vs PM billing menus at your site
- Ask your lead which queue owns 30+ day AR vs denials vs rebills
