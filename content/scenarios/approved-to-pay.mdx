## When this applies

Claim finalized to pay but payment not released yet — capture amounts and expected payment timing.

## Call / portal questions

1. What is the processed date?
2. What are allowed, paid, and patient responsibility amounts?
3. When can we expect the payment?
4. May I have the claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Validate allowed = paid + patient responsibility
- Document expected payment timeframe
- Set follow-up for expected release date

## Typical actions

- Need to allow time
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
