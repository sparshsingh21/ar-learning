## When this applies

Payer reports claim paid — verify amounts, check details, and confirm posting.

## Call / portal questions

1. What is the processed and paid date?
2. What are allowed, paid, deductible, coinsurance, and copay?
3. What is the check/EFT number and issue date?
4. May I have the claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Match ERA/EOB to payer statements
- Confirm payment reached the provider
- Work underpayments via paid incorrectly scenario if variance exists

## Typical actions

- Payment posted / confirmed
- Need to allow time for payment posting
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
