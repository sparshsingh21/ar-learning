## When this applies

Payment applied to prior overpayment/offset — document offset details and remaining balance.

## Call / portal questions

1. What amount was paid on this claim?
2. What amount was taken as offset and for which claim?
3. Is a demand letter or recovery detail available?
4. May I have the claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Identify original overpayment claim tied to offset
- Confirm whether offset is valid
- Escalate disputed offsets per client SOP

## Typical actions

- Offset accepted / documented
- Need client assistance
- Need to submit an appeal

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
