## When this applies

Payer shows no claim received — verify coverage, TFL, payer ID, and resubmit or fax with POTF.

## Call / portal questions

1. May I have policy effective and termed date?
2. Is the policy active on the DOS?
3. What is the timely filing limit?
4. May I have claim mailing address, Payer ID, and Fax#?
5. Can we fax or mail the claim along with POTF?
6. May I get the call reference number?

## Research checklist

- Confirm clearinghouse acceptance vs rejection first
- Verify eligibility and COB on DOS
- Confirm TFL and gather POTF if past TFL
- Resubmit, fax, or release to patient per findings

## Typical actions

- Resubmitted the claim
- Claim released to patient
- Faxed the claim
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
