## When this applies

Claim is pending payer processing — check received date, TAT, and delay reasons.

## Call / portal questions

1. When did you receive the claim?
2. What is the normal processing time or TAT?
3. Is the claim still within TAT?
4. What is the reason for delay?
5. What documents or information are requested?
6. May I have the claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Calculate TAT from received date
- If within TAT, set follow-up for remaining days
- If delayed for provider docs, send or task client
- If delayed for patient info, check letter sent date before releasing

## Typical actions

- Need to allow more time
- Claim released to patient
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
