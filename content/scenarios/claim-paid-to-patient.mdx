## When this applies

Benefits paid to patient/insured — usually AOB or OON; confirm reason and patient billing path.

## Call / portal questions

1. What is the processed and paid date?
2. What are allowed, paid, and patient responsibility amounts?
3. Why was the claim paid to the patient?
4. May I have the claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Do not bill secondary solely because primary paid patient
- Release/balance to patient per SOP
- Review AOB / OON root cause to prevent repeats

## Typical actions

- Claim released to patient
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
