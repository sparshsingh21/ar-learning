## Professional billing (PB)

Bills the **physician or other practitioner’s** professional service — office visits, procedures, interpretations.

- Paper: **CMS-1500**
- Electronic: **837P**
- Uses **Place of Service (POS)** codes
- Reimbursed mainly via **fee schedules** (CPT/HCPCS + modifiers + diagnosis)

## Hospital / facility billing (HB)

Bills the **facility’s** resources — room, nursing, OR, lab, radiology, drugs, supplies.

- Paper: **UB-04 (CMS-1450)**
- Electronic: **837I**
- Uses **revenue codes** for departments/services
- Inpatient often paid via **DRG**; many outpatient hospital services via **APC / OPPS**

## Same visit, two claims

A cardiologist reading an ECG in a hospital outpatient department may generate:

1. Professional claim for the physician service
2. Facility claim for equipment, staff, and hospital resources

## CPT vs ICD-10-PCS

- Professional claims report procedures with **CPT/HCPCS**
- Facility outpatient also commonly uses CPT/HCPCS
- Facility **inpatient** procedures use **ICD-10-PCS**
