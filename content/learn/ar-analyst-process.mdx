## Three phases

### 1. Analyze

Before calling anyone:

- Confirm patient, DOS, payer, claim number, billed amount, and balance
- Read prior notes and tasks
- Check if the denial is typically **non-callable** (coding, duplicates, etc.) per client rules
- Identify timely filing risk

### 2. Research

- Portal / clearinghouse claim status
- ERA/EOB remark codes
- Eligibility or COB if relevant
- Phone call only when the portal cannot answer the decision you need

Keep scenario question lists open so calls stay short and complete.

### 3. Action

Examples:

- Correct and resubmit
- Appeal with records
- Task coding / credentialing / registration
- Bill secondary / tertiary
- Transfer balance to patient
- Schedule follow-up with a clear date

## Note pattern

Write notes so another analyst can continue without re-doing your work:

```
Analysis: [what you see on the claim / denial]
Research: [portal status, call ref #, who you spoke with, answers]
Action: [what you did in the system]
Next: [follow-up date or owning team]
```

## Non-callable mindset

Many coding and duplicate denials should be **routed**, not called. Always check client SOP first — some clients still require a call for confirmation.
