## What TFL means

**Timely filing** is the deadline by which a claim (or corrected claim / appeal, depending on payer rules) must reach the payer. Miss it and payment can be permanently denied even if the service was valid.

Limits vary by payer and plan — examples often cited include ~90 or 180 days for some commercial plans and up to one year for many Medicare Part B claims. **Never assume; verify for that payer.**

## AR priorities

- Work claims approaching TFL first
- Document proof of timely submission (clearinghouse acceptance, payer claim #, certified mail, portal timestamps)
- If payer says “no claim received,” search alternate claim numbers, Tax ID, NPI, and member ID formats before accepting a TFL denial

## Corrected claims and appeals

Filing limits for **original claims**, **corrected claims**, and **appeals** can differ. Read the denial and payer policy carefully before concluding the account is dead.
