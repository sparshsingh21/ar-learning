## Debit vs credit in simple terms

- **Positive (debit) balance** — money still expected
- **Negative (credit) balance** — more money was applied than the remaining charge warrants

## Common causes

1. **Posting error** — wrong payment, duplicate posting, or incorrect adjustment
2. **Duplicate payer payment**
3. **Retroactive eligibility / COB change** after payment
4. **Corrected claim** reducing charges after payment posted
5. **Patient paid** while insurance also paid the same portion

## What to do

1. Recalculate billed / allowed / paid / adjustments (try the [Payment Validator](/tools/payment-validator))
2. Compare to the ERA/EOB line by line
3. Identify whether refund is due to payer or patient
4. Follow client credit-balance SOP (many sites have a dedicated CB team)

Do not write off or refund until the root cause is clear and approved under policy.
