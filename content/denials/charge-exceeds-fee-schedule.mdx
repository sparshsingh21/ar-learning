## When this applies

Contractual reduction or fee schedule cap — verify if true denial vs adjustment.

## Call / portal questions

1. What allowed amount was applied?
2. Is this a contractual adjustment or a true denial?
3. Is underpayment vs contract suspected?
4. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Often informational with payment — confirm posting
- Appeal only when allowed is below contract

## Typical actions

- Accepted contractual adjustment
- Need to submit an appeal
- Appeal sent
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
