## When this applies

Medical necessity denial — gather clinical documentation and appeal.

## Call / portal questions

1. What LCD/NCD or policy was applied?
2. What documents are required for appeal?
3. What is the appeal limit and submission mode?
4. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Collect supporting clinical records
- File appeal within deadline
- Involve clinicians when documentation is weak

## Typical actions

- Need client assistance
- Need to submit an appeal
- Appeal sent
- Tasked coding team
- Write off the claim

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
