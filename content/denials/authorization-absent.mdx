## When this applies

Prior auth missing/invalid — reprocess with auth#, retro auth, or appeal/write-off.

## Call / portal questions

1. May I get the denial date?
2. Is an Auth# available on your system or hospital claim?
3. Can you reprocess using Auth# on file?
4. Was this an emergency (POS 23) service?
5. Is retro authorization possible and what is the procedure?
6. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- If auth exists, ask for reprocess or corrected claim
- Emergency POS may not require auth — confirm with payer
- Follow retro auth procedure or appeal/write-off path

## Typical actions

- Updated the Auth# and resubmitted the claim
- Need to allow time
- Need client assistance
- Need to submit an appeal
- Appeal sent
- Write off the claim

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
