## When this applies

Benefit max met for CPT/period — confirm remaining benefits and patient liability.

## Call / portal questions

1. Which benefit maximum was applied?
2. What is the benefit period and units used?
3. Is any remaining benefit available?
4. Should balance go to secondary or patient?
5. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Confirm max vs billed units/frequency
- Bill secondary if available after primary adjudication
- Otherwise release patient responsibility

## Typical actions

- Claim released to patient
- Billed secondary
- Need client assistance
- Need to submit an appeal

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
