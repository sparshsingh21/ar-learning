## When this applies

Payer says WC is liable — confirm WC claim and bill correct carrier.

## Call / portal questions

1. Is there an open WC claim for this injury?
2. What is the WC carrier and claim number?
3. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Bill WC with injury details
- Do not keep chasing medical plan if WC is confirmed primary

## Typical actions

- Billed other payer
- Need client assistance
- Need to submit an appeal

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
