## When this applies

Service not covered under patient plan or provider contract.

## Call / portal questions

1. Is it non-covered under patient plan or provider contract?
2. What is the specific reason?
3. Has the same CPT paid before for this provider?
4. What is the appeal limit and submission mode?
5. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Route DX issues to coding when plan excludes diagnosis
- Check prior payment history for contract disputes
- Appeal, bill other payer, or release to patient

## Typical actions

- Resubmitted the claim
- Claim released to patient
- Need client assistance
- Need coding assistance
- Need to submit an appeal
- Appeal sent

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
