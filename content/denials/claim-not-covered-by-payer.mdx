## When this applies

Wrong plan/product billed — identify correct payer and rebill.

## Call / portal questions

1. Which plan should have been billed?
2. Is the patient eligible elsewhere on DOS?
3. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Update plan and payer ID
- Bill correct carrier
- Release to patient if no other coverage

## Typical actions

- Billed other payer
- Updated demographics and resubmitted
- Claim released to patient
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
