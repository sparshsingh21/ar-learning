## When this applies

Secondary lacks primary identity/payment information — supply primary details/EOB.

## Call / portal questions

1. What primary payer ID and claim number are required?
2. Do you need the primary EOB attached?
3. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Submit primary EOB and payer details
- Confirm crossover vs manual secondary billing

## Typical actions

- Billed secondary with primary EOB
- Need client assistance
- Need to allow time

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
