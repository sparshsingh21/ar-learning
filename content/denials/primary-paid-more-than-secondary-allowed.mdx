## When this applies

Secondary pays $0 because primary payment meets/exceeds secondary allowed.

## Call / portal questions

1. What was primary paid and secondary allowed?
2. Is any patient responsibility remaining?
3. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Usually expected outcome — confirm math
- Close secondary balance per posting rules

## Typical actions

- Payment posted / confirmed
- Claim released to patient
- Need client assistance
