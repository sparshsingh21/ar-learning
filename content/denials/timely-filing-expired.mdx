## When this applies

TFL denial — gather POTF, appeal if timely, otherwise write off per policy.

## Call / portal questions

1. What timely filing limit is being applied and from which date?
2. Do you show any earlier claim number or acceptance?
3. What proof of timely filing do you accept?
4. What is the appeal limit and address/fax?
5. May I have the claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Compare first submission proof to payer TFL clock
- Appeal with POTF when within original filing window
- Write off when truly late and no exception

## Typical actions

- Need to submit an appeal
- Appeal sent
- Write off the claim
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
