## When this applies

Routine/screening service not a covered benefit for plan.

## Call / portal questions

1. Is there a preventive benefit that should apply?
2. Was the wrong CPT or DX used for screening?
3. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Coding reviews screening vs diagnostic coding
- Patient liability if truly routine non-covered

## Typical actions

- Need coding assistance
- Claim released to patient
- Need to submit an appeal
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
