## When this applies

E/M or service level not supported — may be coding, medical necessity, or records.

## Call / portal questions

1. Is the issue coding level, medical necessity, or records?
2. What documents are required?
3. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Check remark codes for true root cause
- Route to coding or send MR / appeal accordingly

## Typical actions

- Tasked coding team
- Need to submit an appeal
- MR sent
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
