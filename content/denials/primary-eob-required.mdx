## When this applies

Secondary needs primary EOB/payment info before adjudicating.

## Call / portal questions

1. Did primary crossover automatically?
2. What primary payment and patient liability should cross?
3. What address/fax/portal is used to send primary EOB?
4. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Attach primary EOB and resubmit/reopen secondary
- Confirm primary posting is complete first

## Typical actions

- Billed secondary with primary EOB
- Need to allow time
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
