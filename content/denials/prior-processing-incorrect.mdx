## When this applies

Payer indicates prior processing data wrong — clarify and reprocess/appeal.

## Call / portal questions

1. What prior processing data is considered incorrect?
2. Can the claim be reprocessed with corrected information?
3. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Supply corrected data
- Request reprocess or appeal

## Typical actions

- Need to allow time
- Need to submit an appeal
- Appeal sent
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
