## When this applies

Payment issued to a different provider — verify NPI and request reprocess/refund path.

## Call / portal questions

1. Which NPI/Tax ID was paid?
2. Can payment be reissued/reprocessed to the correct provider?
3. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Confirm correct billing provider
- Request void/reissue or corrected claim path

## Typical actions

- Need to allow time
- Need to submit an appeal
- Need client assistance
- Resubmitted the claim

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
