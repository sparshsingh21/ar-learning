## When this applies

Payer sees a prior adjudication — find original claim and work that outcome.

## Call / portal questions

1. What is the original claim number?
2. What is the original claim status (paid/denied)?
3. Are DOS, CPT, and units identical?
4. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Work the original claim, not only the duplicate
- Void/adjust duplicate when original is correct
- Appeal original if underpaid or wrongly denied

## Typical actions

- Adjusted off the claim
- Need to submit an appeal
- Appeal sent
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
