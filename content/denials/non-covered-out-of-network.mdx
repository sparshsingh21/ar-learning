## When this applies

OON denial — confirm network status, OON benefits, and patient liability.

## Call / portal questions

1. Is the rendering/billing provider out of network for this plan?
2. Are out-of-network benefits available?
3. Was a gap exception or single-case agreement used?
4. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Verify enrollment/network roster
- If OON benefits exist, confirm patient coinsurance
- Otherwise patient liability or appeal with exception docs

## Typical actions

- Claim released to patient
- Need client assistance
- Need to submit an appeal
- Appeal sent

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
