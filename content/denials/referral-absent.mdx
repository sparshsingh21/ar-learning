## When this applies

Missing referral from PCP/referring provider — obtain referral or appeal.

## Call / portal questions

1. Was a referral required for this specialty/CPT?
2. Is a referral number on file?
3. Can the claim be reprocessed with the referral#?
4. What is the appeal limit if referral cannot be obtained?
5. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Locate referral in records/portal
- Corrected claim or reprocess when number exists
- Patient liability or appeal when referral cannot be obtained

## Typical actions

- Updated referral and resubmitted
- Need to allow time
- Need client assistance
- Need to submit an appeal
- Appeal sent
- Claim released to patient

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
