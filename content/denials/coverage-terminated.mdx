## When this applies

Policy inactive on DOS — find other coverage or release to patient.

## Call / portal questions

1. What are the effective and termed dates?
2. Is there another active policy on DOS?
3. May I have the correct member ID if coverage moved?
4. May I get the claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Confirm termed date vs DOS
- Bill other active payer if found
- Otherwise transfer to patient per SOP

## Typical actions

- Billed other payer
- Claim released to patient
- Updated demographics and resubmitted
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
