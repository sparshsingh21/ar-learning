## When this applies

ICD does not support CPT — coding/medical necessity review.

## Call / portal questions

1. Which diagnosis and CPT combination was denied?
2. What supporting DX would allow payment?
3. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Route to coding for alternative DX if documented
- Appeal with records when clinical support exists

## Typical actions

- Tasked coding team
- Need coding assistance
- Need to submit an appeal
- Resubmitted the claim
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
