## When this applies

Plan excludes pre-existing condition — confirm policy terms.

## Call / portal questions

1. What look-back period applies?
2. Is there other coverage that pays?
3. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Confirm exclusion language
- Bill other payer or patient

## Typical actions

- Claim released to patient
- Billed other payer
- Need to submit an appeal
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
