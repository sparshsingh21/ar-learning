## When this applies

Hospice election affects coverage — confirm hospice dates and billable services.

## Call / portal questions

1. What are the hospice election dates?
2. Is the billed service related to the hospice diagnosis?
3. Which provider should be billed for this service?
4. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Confirm whether service is hospice-related
- Route to correct payer/provider when appropriate
- Appeal unrelated services with documentation

## Typical actions

- Need client assistance
- Need to submit an appeal
- Appeal sent
- Write off the claim
- Claim released to patient

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
