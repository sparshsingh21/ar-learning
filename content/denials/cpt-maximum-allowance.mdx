## When this applies

Frequency/MUE limit reached for CPT in a period.

## Call / portal questions

1. What is the allowed frequency and period?
2. How many units already paid in the period?
3. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Confirm count vs policy
- Appeal with records if medically necessary beyond limit
- Otherwise patient or write-off per SOP

## Typical actions

- Claim released to patient
- Need to submit an appeal
- Appeal sent
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
