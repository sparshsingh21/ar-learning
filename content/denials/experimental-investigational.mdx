## When this applies

Payer deems service experimental — appeal with literature/policy.

## Call / portal questions

1. Which policy labels the service experimental?
2. What documentation is required for appeal?
3. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Collect clinical justification
- Appeal or patient liability

## Typical actions

- Need to submit an appeal
- Appeal sent
- Claim released to patient
- Need client assistance
- Write off the claim

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
