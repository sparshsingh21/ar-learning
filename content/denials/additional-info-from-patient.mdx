## When this applies

Payer waiting on patient-supplied information — confirm letter status and next steps.

## Call / portal questions

1. What information was requested from the patient?
2. Was a letter sent and on what date?
3. How long will the claim pend waiting for patient response?
4. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Allow reasonable wait after letter sent per client SOP
- Release to patient when wait window expires

## Typical actions

- Need to allow more time
- Claim released to patient
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
