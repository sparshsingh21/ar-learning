## When this applies

Capitated arrangement — confirm if fee-for-service billing is appropriate.

## Call / portal questions

1. Is the provider capitated for this plan on DOS?
2. Should this CPT be carved out of capitation?
3. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Confirm capitation contract vs claim type
- Write off or patient bill only per contract SOP

## Typical actions

- Adjusted off the claim
- Claim released to patient
- Need client assistance
- Need to submit an appeal

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
