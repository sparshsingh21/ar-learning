## When this applies

Member match failed — incorrect name, DOB, gender, or policy ID.

## Call / portal questions

1. Could you search by name, DOB, or SSN?
2. What demographic was incorrect?
3. May I have the correct policy ID?
4. Is the claim on file under the corrected member?
5. May I get the call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Correct demographics and resubmit when match is found
- Release to patient if no active coverage on DOS
- For Medicare, verify MBI and name order carefully

## Typical actions

- Claim released to patient
- Updated the correct patient name and resubmitted the claim
- Updated the correct DOB and resubmitted the claim
- Updated the correct Policy ID and resubmitted the claim
- Need client assistance

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
