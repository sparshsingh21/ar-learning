## When this applies

COB order wrong — bill correct primary then secondary.

## Call / portal questions

1. What is the COB order effective on DOS?
2. Who is the correct primary payer?
3. Has primary already processed?
4. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Update COB and bill correct primary
- Forward primary remit to secondary afterward

## Typical actions

- Billed correct primary
- Billed secondary with primary EOB
- Need client assistance
- Need to submit an appeal

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
