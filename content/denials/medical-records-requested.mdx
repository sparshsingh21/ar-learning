## When this applies

Chart notes/op report requested — send via fax/mail/portal within time limit.

## Call / portal questions

1. May I get the denial date?
2. What is the Fax# or mailing address to send MR?
3. How much is the time limit to send the records?
4. May I have the claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Calculate deadline from denial date
- Send only requested records labeled with claim#
- Confirm receipt when possible

## Typical actions

- Need client assistance
- Need to submit an appeal
- MR sent
- Adjusted off the claim

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
