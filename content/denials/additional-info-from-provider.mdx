## When this applies

Payer needs documents from provider — send within time limit or write off.

## Call / portal questions

1. May I get the denial date?
2. What documents are requested?
3. What is the fax# or mailing address?
4. How much time is allowed to send documents?
5. May I have claim# and call ref#?

## Research checklist

- Send available documents before deadline
- Task client when documents are not on hand
- Write off only when limit passed and client allows

## Typical actions

- Need client assistance
- Need to submit an appeal
- Requested document sent
- Adjusted off the claim

## Notes

Always follow your client SOP. Remark codes can change the true root cause — open the matching scenario if the payer explanation differs from the denial title.
